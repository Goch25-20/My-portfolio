My journey

From the classroom to consulting and student leadership.

My path began in education. In 2022 I volunteered as a tutor at Mabil Cyber Center, training students on Microsoft Word, Excel and PowerPoint, internet browsing and email. I taught them operating systems and file management, made sure they practiced what they learned, and guided them to think critically about technology and solve problems.

From 2023 to 2024 I taught Science and Mathematics to grades 7 and 8 at Amazing Grace Primary School. I enforced school rules firmly, and cases of misconduct fell by more than 70%. As math patron, I coached mathematics competitions, and 25 young mathematicians reached the final rounds of the district middle school competitions. In every class I worked to build critical thinking and communication skills.

In 2025 I began my BSc in Entrepreneurial Leadership at ALU. Since then I have moved into program work, consulting and student leadership, carrying the same habits from the classroom: prepare well, explain clearly and help people grow.
